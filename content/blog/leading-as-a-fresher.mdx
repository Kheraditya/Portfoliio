Starting a career is usually about learning the ropes, but sometimes opportunities arise that demand you to step up immediately. In my case, I transitioned from a fresh recruit to a project owner within just two months.

Here is how I navigated leading end-to-end development and managing a team early in my career.

## 🚀 Rapid Growth and Ownership

Within two months of joining, I was promoted to **Project Owner**. This wasn't just a title change; it meant full responsibility for the project's success.

- **End-to-End Development**: I led the development of a complex backend application using **.NET 8, C#, Node.js, and SQL**.
- **System Design**: I designed **RESTful APIs**, implemented robust business logic layers, and architected **scalable database schemas**.

## 👥 Team Management and Mentorship

Leadership is as much about people as it is about code.

- **Mentoring**: I managed and mentored a team of **3–4 developers**, overseeing task allocation and conducting rigorous code reviews.
- **Sprint Management**: I took charge of sprint planning and delivery updates, ensuring the team maintained velocity and quality.

## 🤝 Client Focus & Production Reliability

Bridging the gap between code and business value.

- **Client Communication**: I handled requirement gathering and direct communication with clients to ensure our solutions met their needs.
- **Production Support**: I was responsible for performance optimization, bug fixes, and resolving critical production issues.
